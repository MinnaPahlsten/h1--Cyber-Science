## c) Threat modeling quickrun
Example Company ->Northern Games which develops and operates an online multiplayer game with in-game purchases.
### Crown jewels
Data->Player accounts and account information

Business continuity->Online game servers must remain available

Reputation->Players need to trust that accounts and purchases are safe

Money->Game sales, subscriptions and in-game purchases
### Bad guys – COI
Capability->Attackers have tools and skills to target online services.

Opportunity->The game and player accounts are available online.

Intent->To steal accounts or disrupt the game.

### Risk 1
Player accounts are hacked.

Likelihood-> 25

Impact->100.000

Estimated risk value->25.000

### Risk 2
A cyberattack takes the game servers offline.

Likelihood->15%

Impact-> 300.000

Estimated risk value-> 45.000

### Biggest risk
Game servers become unavailable.
Risk treatment-> Mitigate
Service disruption has the higher estimated risk value. The company could reduce the risk through protection against attacks, monitoring and recovery planning
